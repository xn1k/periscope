Periscope: third-party notices
==============================

Periscope is made by xnik with Claude (Anthropic) writing the code. It never sends anything into
the game and never reads the game's memory: the addon paints a strip of coloured blocks at the
bottom-left of the screen, and this program reads those pixels and serves a page to your phone on
your own network.

Quest database (gamedata/quests.json, npcs.json, objects.json, items.json)
  Decoded from QuestieDB's World of Warcraft Forever data, https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB

  their own source. Licence text: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.txt

World map tiles (gamedata/maptiles)
  Built from OpenMap's stitched world map art (OpenMap's code is MIT licensed). The art itself is
  Blizzard Entertainment's map art, as OpenMap ships it. Zephras Isle's map, World of Warcraft's
  fonts and item icons are read from your own game files at runtime and are not included.
